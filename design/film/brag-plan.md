# Vikkaa: "How we sell it" film. Creative brief and storyboard

## The prompt (creative direction)

> A 29-second brand film for Vikkaa.lk, a two-person team in Sri Lanka that sells property and vehicles the way global brands launch products.
>
> The story is the change from a phone photo and a classified ad to a campaign. It opens on the cliché: a grey, tilted, flash-lit phone photo of a villa in a cramped "FOR SALE" ad. With one camera-shutter cut, the same villa becomes cinema: golden light, a slow dolly, a lens flare, film grain, a viewfinder settling.
>
> The music lifts and the Vikkaa tick draws itself in light. The film then walks through how Vikkaa works, as five confident beats on the musical grid, each one showing the real thing instead of describing it:
> 1. **The intake form.** Its fields type themselves in.
> 2. **The market report.** Comparable sales rise into a recommended price.
> 3. **The shoot.** A REC viewfinder, a drone HUD climbing over the roof and an interior dolly.
> 4. **Launch day.** One property on five phone screens in 3D: TikTok, Reels, YouTube, Facebook and the portals, each lighting up LIVE.
> 5. **The client portal.** Enquiries, a booked viewing and an offer, ending on a decisive "Sold" tick.
>
> It closes on the wordmark and one line: "Get a marketing plan · vikkaa.lk".

**Look.** Editorial and cinematic:
- deep-green night (`#03150f`, `#0b281e`) against warm paper (`#f6f5f1`);
- Instrument Serif headlines with one italic mint word (`#b5e8cd`), Hanken Grotesk for interface text;
- the brand's diagonal green glow;
- real Sri Lankan photography (Ahangama, Unawatuna, Mirissa, Colombo);
- physical camera language: dollies, parallax, rack-focus blur, whip-pans with motion blur, light leaks and a subtle anamorphic flare.

**No AI slop.** No glowing blobs, gradient meshes, stock-footage montage, generic SaaS copy or invented statistics.

**Sound.** One produced, uplifting track, mixed like a finished edit, with sound design in the same space:
- a shutter on the transformation;
- soft air whooshes on whip-pans;
- real keypresses on the form;
- warm thuds when cards land;
- soft drops as the LIVE badges pop;
- a resonant bell on the logo and on "Sold".

**Format.** Landscape 1920×1080 and vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, 29.5s.

## Facts the video may state

These are true, from the site and spec:
- Vikkaa prices with real market data, films with its own crew (Sony cameras, DJI drones), runs every platform at once and handles every buyer until the deed is signed.
- A three-minute form, and a call back within one working day.
- Nothing is paid until the client approves the plan.
- Western Province.

Illustrative interface text (form values, comparable bars, portal events) is shown as product-in-use and is not a claim. No invented counts, testimonials or client names.

## Music

- **Track:** "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 12" by Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0). The credit goes on the site.
- **Tempo:** 109.96 BPM. One beat = 0.5457s; one bar (4 beats) = 2.1827s; downbeats at 0, 2.18, 4.37, 6.55, 8.73, 10.91, 13.10, 15.28, 17.46, 19.64, 21.83, 24.01, 26.19, 28.37.
- **Energy:** a quiet intro (around −21 dB) until bar 4. It lifts at **8.73s**, then builds to its peak from about 20s.
- **Every cut lands on a downbeat or beat.** The track fades out over 28.4–29.5s.

## Rendering

Each frame is a pure function of time: a paused GSAP timeline, seeked per frame, plus a three.js scene rendered from `t`. Chrome for Testing runs headless through the DevTools protocol (no installs). JPEG frames are piped into ffmpeg (H.264, yuv420p). Audio is mixed with ffmpeg and then muxed. The best settled frame becomes the poster and frame 0. Film grain is added on the website as a CSS overlay, not baked in, to keep the file small.
