# Defense rebuttal

**1. Runway: survives in part.** The record has no sale timeline either way, but the design takes cash before commission through the upfront fee and monthly dealer retainers. The fee is "not decided yet", which is a choice the founders can make now, not a flaw in the model.

**2. Trust: partly survives.** The cycle is real until one case study exists. The record offers agency experience, owned premium gear and a free price read for every no-go, but says nothing about founding-client terms, and those terms are the way out.

**3. Workload: survives against the six-month target, not the start.** "6–8 property campaigns" is a six-month goal, and nothing requires that load in December. Scope is already narrowed: Western Province, property first, vehicles only through dealers and premium cars.

**4. Machinery: fails, except the agreement.** A phone number, provider keys and bank details are setup tasks, not missing capabilities, and the record shows no campaign step that waits on API approval. The lawyer-drafted agreement is a real gap: no exclusive should be signed without it, and the record does not say who pays for it.

**5. Repeat failure: fails.** The first attempt was a marketplace that needed buyers and sellers at once. This is a service where one signed client produces revenue. The platform is already live, and the record schedules selling for December, not more building.

**Deciding point: 1.** If an upfront fee or retainer comes in within three months, the other gaps can be fixed. If none does, the other gaps stop mattering.
