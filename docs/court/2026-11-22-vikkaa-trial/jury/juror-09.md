VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As described, a family would sign away their home for 60-90 days, owe commission "no matter who found the buyer", stay tied by a 6-month tail and send an upfront fee by bank transfer to a firm with no sales and no name, while the agreement meant to protect both sides has not been written and the founders' own households have under 3 months of money left.
DECIDING FACT: "A lawyer is to draft the agreement wording": clients would be asked to sign an exclusive and pay before the contract that sets out their rights and obligations exists.
WOULD FLIP IF: The lawyer-drafted agreement were already in hand and the case were scoped to Western Province property, with no "dominates in Sri Lanka" or international claim, before any seller is asked to sign or pay.
