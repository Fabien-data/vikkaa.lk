# Opening: Defense

The plan fits the founders' real position: no money, no clients, a short clock.

1. **The money model fits zero capital.** The client "pays an upfront fee plus a separate ad budget", and "The founders never pay a client's ads themselves." The gear is "all already owned". Each signed client brings cash before any sale, which answers "Runway: under 3 months." Missing: the fee is "not decided yet", and that number decides whether one client covers a month.

2. **It fixes the first failure.** The earlier "self-serve listings site" produced "an empty marketplace that never launched." This one is "not a listings site", so it needs no two-sided crowd: one signed seller is already a live campaign. Top-priority clients are "repeat sellers — car dealers, land developers selling plots", plus "Dealer retainers: a monthly fee", so one relationship can mean recurring revenue.

3. **The hard-to-buy pieces exist.** Both founders "work in digital marketing and content creation" and came from "a reputed digital marketing agency." The platform is live: "intake form with fit score, pipeline board, proposal/agreement/invoice PDFs, signing with one-time code, evidence log". "No track record" is real, but the founding-client campaign planned for "the first week of December 2026" can prove production quality.

4. **The commission is protected against being cut out.** The deal is "exclusive for 60–90 days", commission is due "no matter who found the buyer", a roughly 6-month follow-on clause applies, and a "tamper-evident evidence log" backs it. Enquiries go to Vikkaa's phone, "never the seller's". The fit score checks "document status, all owners agreeing", so founder time goes to sellable assets.

5. **Focus avoids unwinnable fights.** "Western Province first. Property is the core." Ordinary cars are skipped: "CarConnect.lk and Park & Go already cover ordinary private cars."

**Concessions:** "Dominates in Sri Lanka", international reach and "several markets at once" cannot be defended now; only the Western Province property plan can. The target of 10+ sales in 6 months has no evidence behind it. The record is silent on whether Sri Lankan sellers will pay an upfront fee or sign an exclusive.
