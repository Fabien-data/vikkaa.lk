# Prosecution opening

**1. The money arrives after the runway runs out.** "Runway: under 3 months." "Clients: none yet." Founding-client work starts "the first week of December 2026." Commission "is collected at deed signing or vehicle transfer," and the upfront fee is "not decided yet." Every property must pass intake, call, visit, proposal, signing, shoot, edit, approval and a campaign before commission is owed. The record has no evidence that one sale can close before February. Cost: "one of them has to take other work," and the crew becomes one person.

**2. It asks strangers for the most trust before earning any.** A seller must sign an exclusive, pay "an upfront fee plus a separate ad budget, by bank transfer," owe commission "no matter who found the buyer," and stay bound by a 6-month follow-on clause. The firm asking has "no sales, no case studies" and "no known name." The top-priority clients are car dealers, developers and "independent agents," who already sell on their own. Cost: no signings means no case study, and no case study means no signings.

**3. Two unpaid people cannot do the job as written.** The founders are "the entire crew": visits, shooting, editing, answering "every buyer enquiry," viewings, test drives, negotiation. One also builds the software. The target is "6–8 property campaigns plus several car campaigns a month," plus "several markets at once." Cost: slow replies and lost buyers, the exact failure "Clients never have to ask 'what's happening?'" is meant to prevent.

**4. The machinery is not ready.** Still missing: "the real business phone number, SMS and email provider keys, a price guide, bank details." Meta/WhatsApp, TikTok and YouTube approvals "have not been completed." The agreement protecting commission still awaits a lawyer "to draft" it. Cost: campaigns and commission claims rest on parts that do not exist.

**5. It repeats the last failure.** The first attempt "produced an empty marketplace that never launched." This attempt again built a full platform before one client agreed to work with them. Cost: a second polished system with nothing going through it.
