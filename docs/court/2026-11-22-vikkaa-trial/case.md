The founders believe Vikkaa.lk can succeed on revenue and finance, win big clients, and earn trust in both the local market and the international market (starting with Sri Lankans living abroad). They want to become the trusted names in the field, the way top realtors and car dealers in Sri Lanka have built strong personal brands that convince people they are capable. ## What the business is (from the founders' own design document, dated 2026-11-21)

Vikkaa.lk is a done-for-you marketing and sales brand for properties and vehicles in Sri Lanka. It is **not a listings site**: sellers cannot post ads themselves. The flow:

1. A seller fills an online intake form; the system gives a fit score (reason for selling, price vs market, document status, all owners agreeing, willingness to sign exclusive, location).
2. A 20–30 minute discovery call.
3. A site visit by a founder, ending in go / no-go. A no-go still gets a short free price read.
4. A proposal: market report with comparable prices and recommended asking price, content plan, quote, and an exclusive agreement.
5. The client signs (one-time code to phone, NIC captured, every co-owner signs) and pays an upfront fee plus a separate ad budget, by bank transfer.
6. The founders shoot premium content themselves (2 Sony Alpha cameras, 2 DJI drones, lighting, flagship phones — all already owned), edit it, and the client approves it in an online portal.
7. The campaign runs from Vikkaa's own Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Meta ad account, paid from the client's ad deposit. The founders never pay a client's ads themselves. Copy-ready kits for ikman, LankaPropertyWeb and Riyasewana.
8. Vikkaa answers every buyer enquiry on its own phone/WhatsApp (never the seller's), qualifies buyers, runs group viewings and test drives, records offers and negotiates.
9. On sale: commission invoice, with the upfront fee credited if it sells within the agreement period. Commission is collected at deed signing or vehicle transfer. A case study is published.

**Agreement terms:** exclusive for 60–90 days; commission due if the asset sells during that period no matter who found the buyer; follow-on clause — commission still due if a Vikkaa-introduced buyer buys within about 6 months after the agreement ends. An append-only, tamper-evident evidence log records every buyer's first contact, viewing and offer, to defend the commission. A lawyer is to draft the agreement wording.

**Money:** upfront fee set per quote (amount **not decided yet**). Commission starting values: property 3% (about 2% on high-value deals); vehicles a percentage or a minimum amount. Dealer retainers: a monthly fee covering a set number of cars a month. Not VAT-registered at the start (threshold Rs 60M a year).

**Who the clients are, in priority order:** (1) repeat sellers — car dealers, land developers selling plots, small builders and condo projects, independent agents; (2) private property sellers and premium-car owners; (3) Sri Lankans abroad selling property at home.

**Focus:** Western Province first. Property is the core. Vehicles only via dealer retainers and premium cars — the founders note that CarConnect.lk and Park & Go already cover ordinary private cars.

**Buyer community, "Vikkaa Circle":** buyers opt in with recorded consent and see new assets 48 hours before the public, via members-only links and WhatsApp broadcast lists. Referrals are tracked and rewarded when a referred client signs. Must comply with Sri Lanka's data protection law (PDPA): consent, opt-out, export/delete on request.
