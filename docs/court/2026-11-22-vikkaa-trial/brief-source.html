Court brief · 22 November 2026
For the two co-founders
Put on trial: can Vikkaa.lk dominate?

Twelve independent jurors heard a prosecution and a defense, then voted without seeing each other: an accountant, a first-time seller, a competitor, a retired judge, a teenager, an engineer, a small-business owner, a statistician, a parent, an investor, a designer and a lawyer. Court pages are their verdict, quoted unchanged. Plan pages are my analysis built from it.

The verdict
GUILTY, 12 to 0
The case fails as stated. It should not go ahead the way it is described.
The case: "Two co-founders with zero capital will make Vikkaa.lk the best property and vehicle advertising/marketing company: one that dominates in Sri Lanka and is capable of serving international markets as well."

The verdict is on the plan as stated today. Every juror wrote down what would flip their vote, and nearly all of them named the same event: one paying client, under a real contract, before the money runs out.

"Stop building; start selling. Set your price, sign one paying client under a real contract, and claim no more than you can prove."
The judge's sentence

Charges that stuck Court

An exclusive, an upfront fee, commission "no matter who found the buyer" and a 6-month tail, with no case study.

03The success rate: an honest read Plan

There's no honest percentage yet. As the Statistician juror said, every number you'd need is zero, and comparing yourselves to "top realtors and car dealers" "only counts the winners."

As stated

Dominate Sri Lanka and go international, on under 3 months of runway with zero clients. The court says it fails, and I agree.

Narrowed

Done-for-you property sales in the Western Province, plus dealer and developer retainers. A real business with a fighting chance, if one paying client signs before about 31 Jan 2027.

Your first real success rate is pitch-to-paid conversion. Count every pitch. 30 pitches producing 1–2 paying clients means you have a business to grow. 40 pitches producing none means the offer or price is wrong, so change it rather than pitch harder.

04SWOT Plan

Strengths

  • Agency-trained marketing and content: premium production classifieds can't match.
  • All the gear is owned, so shoots cost no capital.
  • A live platform: the portal, weekly reports and evidence log make transparency visible.
  • A service, not a marketplace: one client is revenue.
  • Clients fund their own ads.
  • An AI engineer on the team: a WhatsApp assistant can multiply capacity later.
  • Focus chosen: Western Province, property first.

Weaknesses

  • Zero clients, zero audience, zero track record.
  • Under 3 months of runway.
  • No price set and no contract drafted.
  • Two people do everything, and one also writes the software.
  • A blurred offer: "marketing company" or exclusive sales agent?
  • A habit of building before selling. The first attempt died of it.

Opportunities

  • A market of low-quality classified ads, where premium, honest presentation stands out.
  • Dealers, developers and condo projects: one relationship means monthly retainers.
  • Sri Lankans abroad need someone trusted at home, and the platform is built for that.
  • Every shoot is also founder content: the personal brand comes as a by-product.
  • The Vikkaa Circle buyer list compounds with every campaign.
  • The platform becomes a sellable product once proven.

Obstacles

  • The trust barrier: exclusive, fee, commission "no matter who found the buyer", 6-month tail.
  • Incumbents with names. "Drone footage … I can hire in a week," said the Competitor.
  • Cheap alternatives: ikman, or an agent the seller already knows.
  • Long cycles: commission only at deed signing or transfer.
  • Legal: enforceability, ad-deposit handling, the PDPA.
  • Burnout, or a founder forced back to a job.
  • Ops: pending approvals, and a shared server about 88% full.

06Pricing: two tracks Plan

The jury pulled in two directions. Eight jurors want a fixed fee paid inside the runway, while the First-Time Customer wants no upfront risk. Two tracks satisfy both.

Track 1 · pays the bills

Dealers and developers

  • Dealer retainer: a fixed monthly fee for N cars (shoot, edit, campaign, enquiries), paid in advance.
  • Developer pilot: a fixed price for one project page or plot batch, with 1 month of campaigning, designed to become a retainer.
  • Paid before work starts. That's the cash inside the runway.
Track 2 · builds the proof

2–3 founding private sellers

  • A low or zero upfront fee, commission on sale only, and a shorter exclusive (for example 45–60 days).
  • In return: the right to publish the case study and a video testimonial.
  • The client still pays the ad budget. Cap it at 3, then switch to standard terms.

09Trust and personal brand: the realtor playbook Plan

Top agents and dealers win because they are visible, specific and show proof. You don't have proof yet, so lead with the first two and let the proof build.

Trust basics (done once)

  • A registered business and a business bank account. Never ask for transfers to a personal account.
  • One real phone and WhatsApp number, answered fast.
  • A Google Business Profile, so reviews start from client one.
  • Publish your terms, refund rules and the "if it doesn't sell" policy openly.
  • Show the portal: "You see every enquiry, viewing and rupee of ad spend, live."

One lane per founder

  • The numbers founder (the AI engineer): a weekly "Western Province price read", area price explainers, and how to price right. Authority without a sales record.
  • The storyteller founder (runs social): behind-the-scenes from every shoot, walkthroughs, seller and buyer stories.
  • Both on camera, under your own names, linked to Vikkaa.

Content rhythm, made from work you already do

  • 3 posts a week minimum. Every shoot yields at least one founder post.
  • A free 10-minute price read as the lead magnet (it's already in your design for no-go sellers). Cap it at a few a week.
  • One case study per sale, with the seller on video.
  • Never post a claim you can't back. One real result beats ten big promises.

10Big clients and networking Plan

  • Start warm. Former agency clients and contacts are the shortest path to a first developer or dealer.
  • Pitch with their own ads. Put their current classified listing next to a Vikkaa-style shot of the same kind of asset.
  • Pilot, then retainer. A fixed-price one-month pilot is easy to agree to. Fix the retainer terms in advance.
  • Ask every client for two introductions. Dealers know dealers, and developers know builders. Referral rewards are already in your design.
  • Be in the room: property and vehicle exhibitions, developer launches, dealer associations. Carry the portal demo on your phone.

11Community

The Vikkaa Circle Plan

  • Open sign-ups now, with recorded consent. "We never contact you without permission" is itself a trust message.
  • Members see new assets 48 hours early (already designed).
  • Give value when there's nothing to buy: monthly price reads and buying guides.
  • Target from your design: 1,500 consented buyers by month 6. Track it weekly.

12International

When, not now Plan

  • Not before 3–5 published local case studies. International trust is borrowed from local proof.
  • First market: Sri Lankans abroad selling property at home. Remote signing, live reports and the evidence log are built for them.
  • Channels: relatives of local clients, diaspora groups, referrals.
  • Hold the claim until a real overseas seller has been served.
