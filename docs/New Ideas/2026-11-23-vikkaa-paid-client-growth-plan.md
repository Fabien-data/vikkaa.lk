# Vikkaa.lk: Paid-client growth and platform plan

Prepared for the two founders on 23 November 2026. Scope: done-for-you property and vehicle-dealer marketing and sales in the Western Province.

This report follows the ten-expert brief, using the code review and research across the ten expert perspectives.

**Evidence key:** **[sourced]** means linked evidence; **[estimate]** means a stated calculation; **[assumption]** means a proposed target or unvalidated input. Published competitor prices are asking prices, not proof of what customers actually pay.

## 1. Executive summary

**Decision: sell a bounded, prepaid campaign using the platform you already have.** Focus on owner-run vehicle dealers and small property developers in the Western Province. Accept private sellers who fit the same offer and can fund it.

**One offer**

> **Vikkaa films, markets and manages buyer enquiries and viewing follow-up for Western Province property sellers and vehicle dealers, with a clear record of the work and results.**

The seller approves price and claims, supplies someone to conduct viewings, and makes the final sale decision. Negotiation or brokerage authority must be explicitly agreed.

**Proposed price: LKR 90,000 for a prepaid campaign lasting 30 days [assumption].** Client-funded advertising is separate, capped initially at **LKR 30,000 [assumption]**. Renew only by agreement.

| Included scope | Proposed limit—all figures [assumption] |
|---|---|
| Production | One location; one shoot lasting up to four hours |
| Inventory | Up to four vehicles, or one property/project |
| Content | Four vertical videos and eight photo/carousel posts |
| Distribution | Facebook/Instagram campaign management; suitable content reused elsewhere |
| Conversion | One campaign page; handling up to forty new enquiries; viewing coordination |
| Reporting | Weekly update and a final report |
| Boundaries | Agreed response hours, revision allowance, and client handover for overflow |

**No guaranteed sale.** The fee buys specified work and buyer follow-up. State remedies for undelivered work.

**Proposed change from the earlier repo strategy:** use no compulsory commission or broad exclusive for this initial campaign. Your existing design requires commission; changing that is a commercial decision for the founders. This report recommends the change because prepaid delivery offers a clearer survival test. Existing agreements would retain their signed terms.

**First month:** obtain a permissioned demonstration, finish the service scope, pitch decision-makers, collect payment, and deliver a campaign. Aim for the first cleared payment by **13 December 2026 [assumption]**. Protect selling time; defer broad integrations.

**Single demand-validation metric:** unrelated customers with both a signed scope and a fully cleared service-fee payment. Require **at least one by 31 January 2027 [assumption: deadline required by your brief]**. Free work, ad deposits, unpaid contracts, and founder-funded purchases do not count.

That payment permits another bounded test. It does not establish profitability or justify hiring.

### 7. Legal and compliance advisor

**Decision:** obtain advice on the actual service and enforce permission records before prospect messaging.

The DPA’s official update dated **16 November 2026** states that sections **2 and 3**, Parts **I and III**, commence **1 January 2027**. Parts **II, IV and VII** had no appointed commencement dates in the orders reviewed. **[sourced: [DPA Background](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/Background)]**

The enacted marketing provision requires consent, sender identification and opt-out. Its commencement status does not change your explicit requirement for recorded permission or a valid, documented basis. A public email address and human batch approval are not themselves consent. [PDPA statutory text](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/acts/Data%20Protection%20Act%20SL%20-%20English%20%282%29.pdf)

Practical requirements:

- Separate responding to an enquiry from permission for future promotions.
- Record channel, purpose, source, notice wording, timestamp, withdrawal and approving founder.
- Have counsel determine any alternative lawful basis for the particular communication.
- Define deliverables, media-money treatment, cancellation, remedies, authority and any commission trigger.
- Treat electronic service signing separately from property-transfer formalities. [Electronic Transactions Act](https://www.icta.lk/icta-assets/uploads/2016/03/ElectronicTransactionActNo19of2006.pdf), [property-formality amendment](https://documents.gov.lk/view/acts/2022/10/30-2022_E.pdf)
- Vehicle ownership transfer follows DMT procedures, including forms **MTA 6 and MTA 8 [sourced: [DMT](https://dmt.gov.lk/index.php?Itemid=148&id=24&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article)]**.
- Real-estate buying/selling activity can bring FIU obligations; describing the business as marketing does not settle its classification. [FIU guidance](https://www.fiusrilanka.gov.lk/docs/press_releases/2023/FIU_07_07_2023/Real_Estate_E.pdf)

**Platform checks:** WhatsApp requires opt-in and honours a **24-hour service window [sourced: [policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)]**; do not request full NIC numbers in chat. Google and TikTok lead products require appropriate privacy disclosures. [Google](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9472930), [TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-ad-format-and-functionality). Meta’s current country-specific housing configuration was not verified; check the actual account before launch.

**Confidence:** high on cited official requirements; medium on application to Vikkaa’s agreements and activities.

## 8. Risk register and decision rules

The ranking is a practical hypothesis **[assumption]**.

| Failure | Early warning | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Building replaces selling | Pitch commitments missed while features accumulate | Protect selling blocks; stop discretionary development |
| Customers will not pay | Praise and proposals without cleared fees | Review objections; change targeting or scope |
| Cash expires first | Forecast cannot fund essentials and committed delivery | Cut discretionary spend; activate outside-income/closure plan |
| Inventory is unsuitable | Buyers repeatedly reject price, condition or authority | Correct the issue with the seller or decline the campaign |
| Cheap leads are poor leads | Duplicates, unreachable contacts, no attendance | Review source and qualification before the next ad tranche |
| Scope overwhelms founders | Editing and enquiries displace sales | Enforce scope; route overflow; fund targeted contractor support |
| Permission or privacy failure | Missing basis, ignored opt-out, wrong-client disclosure | Stop affected flow, preserve records and address the incident |
| Claims exceed evidence | “Verified” or performance claims lack support | Narrow wording or obtain the missing evidence |
| Fee/attribution dispute | Client contests introduction, spend or promised work | Use signed scope and timestamped records; resolve promptly |
| No renewal or platform dependency | Clients leave; one account loss stops acquisition | Interview clients, improve delivered value and preserve permissioned records |

**Keep going:** the paid-client gate passes, measured campaign contribution is positive, and available cash covers committed delivery plus the next test **[assumption]**.

**Change:** after **thirty qualified pitches and ten written proposals without payment [assumption]**, inspect the objections. Repair targeting if the wrong people were approached. Revise price, scope or perceived risk if suitable prospects still decline. Permit a **two-week retest only if funded [assumption]**.

**Stop discretionary venture spending:** no qualifying paid client by **31 January 2027 [assumption: required gate]**, or earlier if cash cannot preserve **four weeks of essential household spending plus committed delivery/refund obligations [assumption]**.

**Hire later:** positive contribution survives paid labour costs, clients renew, response and quality stay within the contract, and the cash reserve supports the role. Gross revenue alone is insufficient.
