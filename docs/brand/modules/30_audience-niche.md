# 30 · Audience and niche

Status: **draft from documents and research. The founder interview is still pending.**
Frameworks: Baker "The Business of Expertise", ideal customer profile (ICP).

## Raw

- The design's client priority: "(1) repeat sellers — car dealers, land developers selling plots, small builders and condo projects, independent agents; (2) private property sellers and premium-car owners; (3) Sri Lankans abroad."
- "Western Province first. Property is the core. Vehicles only via dealer retainers and premium cars."
- Court condition 1: "pitch dealers and developers until one signs and pays before the runway ends."
- Research: used Japanese car dealers hold unsold stock after the 2026 duty surcharge. Colombo condo volumes fell 15.2% in Q1 2026. Land prices rose 31.9%. Buyers are local residents buying for their own occupancy with their own funds. Overseas owners fear forgery and distance.

## Synthesis: five segments, in pursuit order

| # | Segment | Pain right now | Trigger to buy | Offer | Proof they need | Where to reach them |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Used and reconditioned car dealers** (Western Province) | Unsold stock after the 2026 duty surcharge; buyers moving to EVs and new Chinese cars | Stock ageing on the lot | **Dealer retainer**: N cars a month filmed, campaigned, enquiries handled; paid monthly in advance | Before/after of one of *their* cars; enquiry count in 2 weeks | Walk-in visits, Riyasewana and ikman dealer pages, dealer associations, Facebook |
| B | **Land developers and small condo or builder projects** | Higher prices, slower sales | A new plot batch or launch, or slow-moving units | **Developer pilot**: one project page plus a 1-month campaign at a fixed price, then a retainer | Project page quality; leads delivered | Developer offices, launch events, LankaPropertyWeb project listings, former agency contacts |
| C | **Motivated private sellers** (Western Province, selling within 6 months, clean documents) | Fear of brokers and scams; their own listing gets no serious buyers | Relocation, inheritance, upgrade, financial need | **Founding terms (first 3 only)**: low or zero upfront fee, commission on sale, shorter exclusive, in exchange for case-study rights | Seeing the portal; the verified process; a founder they can meet | The free price-read lead magnet, Facebook campaigns, referrals |
| D | **Overseas owners** selling property at home | Trust, forged-deed fear, distance, power-of-attorney risk | A family decision or migration | The standard engagement, fully remote: power-of-attorney signing, video walkthroughs, live portal | 3–5 local case studies first | Relatives of local clients, diaspora groups, referrals (**later phase**) |
| E | **Buyers** (the Vikkaa Circle) | Fake listings, wasted viewings | Moving, upgrading | A verified listing, 48-hour early access, and honest answers | Real photos and video, an ID-checked process | Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp Channel; opt-in only |

**Niche focus:** start with A and B for cash (they pay before any sale) and C for proof (case studies). Hold D until the proof exists. E builds from every campaign.

**What to capture in the app per segment** (this feeds the plan): segment, district, stock or units, current listing links, decision-maker, next action, and pitch page.

**Open questions for the founder interview:**
1. Who in your existing network (former agency clients, family, friends) owns a dealership or a development? Name five.
2. Which would you rather spend your days with: dealers or developers? Why?
3. What is the smallest dealer you would take on, in cars a month?
