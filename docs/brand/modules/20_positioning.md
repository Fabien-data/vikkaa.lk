# 20 · Positioning

Status: **draft from documents and research. The founder interview is still pending.**
Frameworks: Dunford "Obviously Awesome", Moore positioning template.

## Raw

- The design says "It is **not a listings site.** Sellers cannot post ads themselves."
- The anti-goal: "anything that feels like a classifieds portal, or makes the brand look cheap."
- Court condition 2 (8 jurors): "One offer: done-for-you property sales in the Western Province."
- The Competitor juror: "Their edge is drone footage and editing, which I can hire in a week. What I actually have, trust and a known name, is the thing they have none of."
- Built and live: the client portal, the append-only evidence log, multi-owner OTP signing with NIC capture, buyer check-in with NIC, and weekly reporting (planned to be automated).

## Synthesis (Dunford's five components)

| Component | Draft |
|---|---|
| **Competitive alternatives** (what sellers do today) | List it themselves on ikman, LankaPropertyWeb or Riyasewana. Give it to a broker they know, or several brokers at once. A developer's in-house sales team. Word of mouth. Do nothing and wait. |
| **Unique attributes** | (1) A verification process: documents checked, every owner signs, buyers ID-checked at viewings, and a tamper-evident record of every buyer. (2) A live client portal showing enquiries, viewings, offers and ad spend. (3) Production done in-house (cinema cameras, drones) by the people who also sell. (4) One accountable team, never a crowd of brokers. |
| **Value** | Sell faster at a fair price without the risk and the noise. You always know what's happening, and your commission pays for a proven result. |
| **Best-fit customers** | Repeat sellers who need stock moved (dealers with unsold cars, developers selling plots or units). Motivated private sellers in the Western Province with clean documents. Overseas owners who can't be present. |
| **Market category** | **"Verified property sales service"**, not "advertising agency" and not "listing site". It frames Vikkaa against brokers (unregulated) and portals (self-serve), on trust. |

The **Competitor's attack** is that production can be copied. The answer is that the moat is the **verified process plus a public track record**, not the footage. The footage is just the proof you can see.

**Moore template:**
> For **property owners and dealers in the Western Province** who **need to sell without gambling on unknown brokers or blurry listings**, **Vikkaa** is a **verified property sales service** that **films, markets and sells your asset while you watch every enquiry, viewing and offer live**. Unlike **classified sites and traditional brokers**, Vikkaa **verifies every step and is paid in full only when it sells.**

**Three candidate one-line offers (for the site hero, outreach and pitch pages):**
1. "We sell your property. You watch every step." (the transparency lead)
2. "Verified property sales in the Western Province: filmed, marketed and sold for you." (the category lead, matching court condition 2)
3. "Your property, properly sold." (short, premium; needs a subline that carries the proof)

**Recommendation:** use 2 as the working positioning, because it is literal and matches the court's narrowed claim. Use 1 as the emotional subline. Revisit after the founder interview.

**Open questions for the founder interview:**
1. Name one peer agency or dealer you admire but would never want to become. What makes them the wrong model?
2. When a seller says "why not just put it on ikman?", what is your answer in one sentence?
3. Is "Verified" a promise you can keep on every asset, including a lawyer's title check? If not, what exactly does Vikkaa verify?
