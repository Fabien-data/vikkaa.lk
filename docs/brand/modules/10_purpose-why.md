# 10 · Purpose / Why

Status: **draft from documents and research. The founder interview is still pending.**
Frameworks: Sinek Golden Circle, Lencioni.

## Raw

From the founders' design document (21 Nov 2026):
> "Property and vehicle marketing in South Asia is mostly low-quality classified ads. The founders want to: bring Western-style marketing to that market; become known personally as the trustworthy specialists for it; use that to establish themselves in the realtor industry; expand abroad later, starting with Sri Lankans living overseas; and eventually sell this platform to others."

> "Clients never have to ask 'what's happening?'"

From founder A (22 Nov 2026):
> "make vikkaa.lk as the best property and vehicle advertising/marketing company … dominates in Sri Lanka and capable to serving international as well"
> "build a community that is trustworthy"
> "most of the realtors and car dealers they have this stronghold where that they have really invest in their personal branding … to convince the people that this guys are capable"

From the court (22 Nov 2026, 12–0 guilty "as stated"):
> "claim no more than you can prove." (judge)
> "A stranger cannot tell in five seconds what Vikkaa is." (Designer)

From the research (see [market research](../research/2026-11-market-research.md)):
- An estimated 40–50% of land deeds are forged, and brokers are unregulated and uncertified.
- Overseas owners delay selling because of trust and distance.

## Synthesis

**Why (the belief underneath):** selling the biggest thing you own shouldn't feel like a gamble. In Sri Lanka today it often does, because of blurry classified ads, unregulated middlemen, forged papers and no visibility into what's happening. The founders' drive sits in two words that recur in every source: **trust** and **proof**.

**How:** every step is visible and verifiable. Real production replaces blurry phone photos, one accountable team replaces a crowd of brokers, and a live record replaces "trust me".

**What:** a done-for-you sales service for property (and dealer vehicles). Vikkaa prices it, films it, campaigns it, handles every buyer, and gets paid when it sells.

**Three candidate purpose statements:**
1. "We make selling property in Sri Lanka something you can verify, not something you have to trust."
2. "Every sale, proven. Every step, visible."
3. "Honest, beautiful, verified: the way property should be sold in Sri Lanka."

**Contradictions to resolve:**
- "Advertising/marketing company" (founder A's words) versus "sales service paid on sale" (the design). The court's Designer juror flagged this blur. The founders need to choose which one they *are* to the client.
- "Dominate Sri Lanka and international" (ambition) versus "claim no more than you can prove" (court). Keep the ambition internal. The public promise is only what's proven.

**Open questions for the founder interview (asked one at a time):**
1. What moment made each of you angry enough about this market to start Vikkaa? Tell the story.
2. Brand obituary: if Vikkaa closed in five years, what would sellers miss most?
3. What would you refuse to do, even for a big commission?
