# Vikkaa paid client launch plan

Prepared for the founders as at 23 November 2026 [sourced: session date]. Scope: Western Province property sellers and vehicle dealers. The starting position is two founders, no clients, no audience and less than three months of runway [sourced: founder brief]. This is a commercial test, not a forecast of success.

## Executive decision

**Sell the service now. Extend the platform only where it helps obtain payment or safely deliver the paid campaign.** Prioritize owner-run dealers and small developers with available inventory, realistic pricing and an accessible decision-maker. Take a private seller only on the same commercial terms. Keep the same offer across both asset categories; let actual paid conversion determine which segment receives more effort [assumption].

**The offer:** “Vikkaa films, markets and manages buyer enquiries and viewing follow-up for Western Province property sellers and vehicle dealers, with a visible record of what happened.” The seller approves price, attends or supplies the person conducting viewings, makes final sale decisions and completes the transfer through the appropriate professionals. Negotiation authority and regulated agency duties require an explicit legal scope; the word “marketing” does not remove them.

**The price:** LKR 90,000 for a prepaid 30-day campaign, renewable at the same fee and scope; applicable taxes separately identified [assumption]. Client-funded media: up to LKR 30,000, paid through the client's own ad account [assumption]. No pilot commission, broad sales exclusive or automatic renewal [assumption]. The service fee pays for work and enquiry handling; it does not buy a guaranteed sale.

**The scope:** one location and one shoot of up to four hours; up to four inventory items or one property project; four edited vertical videos and eight photo/carousel posts; one campaign page; Facebook/Instagram distribution and ad management; handling up to forty new buyer enquiries; appointment coordination; a weekly update and final report [assumption]. State business response hours, revision allowance and the handover procedure for overflow in the signed scope. Overflow is routed to the client, never silently ignored. Extra sites, unlimited filming and physical attendance at every viewing are outside this price. Refund/remedy terms cover agreed work not delivered, with counsel-approved wording.

**First thirty days [assumption]:** prepare a real, permissioned demonstration and written scope; obtain qualified decision-maker conversations; ask for payment; deliver the first campaign. Aim for the first cleared payment by 13 December 2026 [assumption], well before the outer deadline of 31 January 2027 [sourced: founder brief]. Use founder visits, warm introductions and consented follow-up. Spend no Vikkaa cash on acquisition ads before proof [assumption].

**The single demand-validation metric:** number of unrelated customers with a signed scope and a fully cleared service-fee payment. At least one by 31 January 2027 is the minimum continuation gate [assumption: decision rule matching the brief]. An unpaid signature, free trial, media deposit or founder-funded purchase does not count. Passing this gate permits a bounded next test; scaling still requires positive contribution, renewals and sufficient cash. The earlier cash stop always overrides the calendar.

**Immediate choices:** name a sales owner and a delivery owner; publish the precise scope and price; have counsel review the service/data agreement and agency classification; use the existing CRM; fix the outreach permission gap before using its batch sender. Do not wait for audience growth, a new brand film or an integration project [assumption].

### Sales and conversion coach

**Decision:** sell a bounded paid campaign with a demonstration and a visible price. **Confidence: medium on the structure; low on willingness to pay until money clears.**

Published alternatives support testing the price: Ceylond lists **LKR 79,900 monthly** for its growth package with media extra [sourced: [rate card](https://ceylond.com/sri-lanka/social-media-packages)]; Cypher lists **LKR 100,000 monthly** with original filmed reels [sourced: [packages](https://cypherdigital.lk/blog/social-media-marketing-packages)]. These are asking prices, not evidence of dealers' or developers' actual payments. Named property agencies publish success commissions; the competitor table makes that objection explicit.

Use these scripts after permission has been established; attach the actual scope and **LKR 90,000 fee [assumption]** rather than leaving price for a later conversation.

- **Dealer:** “Which available vehicles need attention, and what happens after someone asks about them? We film the agreed stock, run the campaign, qualify enquiries and coordinate viewings. You approve price and availability; you see the enquiry and appointment record. Here is the paid campaign scope and an example report.”
- **Developer:** “Which available units need site visits now? We can explain that inventory clearly, qualify buyer enquiries and coordinate visits with your sales team. Who owns price approval, availability updates and follow-up after the visit?”
- **Private seller:** “We manage your approved presentation, enquiries and viewing coordination. You retain price approval and use your own lawyer for title and transfer. The written fee covers defined work; we do not promise a buyer or receive the purchase deposit.”

Close with an agreed next action and, in any prospect message, “If you do not want further messages from Vikkaa, tell us and we will stop.” Log consent/lawful basis, purpose, channel and the founder approving the send.

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “You have no clients.” | “Correct. This is our first paid campaign. This demonstration is labelled, and here are the founders, scope, approval process and report.” |
| “I can list it cheaply.” | “Use those listings. Our fee covers production, buyer handling and appointment coordination. Compare the defined work and your time.” |

### Legal and compliance advisor

**Decision:** settle the service boundary and data workflow before taking the first campaign live. **Confidence: high on cited commencement/policy facts; medium on their application to Vikkaa.** Obtain Sri Lankan counsel's written review of the actual agreement and activities.

Core PDPA processing/controller duties commence **1 January 2027 [sourced: [Gazette](https://dpa.gov.lk/Gazet/2498-16_E.pdf)]**. The DPA's status at **16 November 2026 [sourced: [DPA background](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/Background)]** says the rights, solicited-message and penalties parts still await appointed dates. Do not say the entire Act is already enforced or becomes effective together. Recheck for later Orders before launch and at the January review.

The enacted solicited-message provisions require consent, sender identity and an opt-out, although that part is not yet commenced. The general processing grounds also include contractual necessity and qualified legitimate interests; this is not blanket marketing permission [sourced: [PDPA text](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/acts/Data%20Protection%20Act%20SL%20-%20English%20%282%29.pdf)]. Follow the stricter founder brief now: consent by default; any alternative needs a documented, channel-specific lawful-basis assessment. Public contact details are not consent. Map controller/processor roles, retention, security, subprocessors and overseas hosting; use the [amended Act](https://dpa.gov.lk/acts/22-2025_E_251104_201549%20%281%29.pdf?v=20261114171535), and do not mistake [draft DPA instruments](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/guidelines) for final rules.

Real estate agents involved in client buying/selling activities fall within FIU's AML/CFT framework. Not holding money does not by itself exempt Vikkaa. Determine the actual classification, customer/beneficial-owner checks, reporting and compliance-officer obligations before offering agency/negotiation services [sourced: [FIU FAQ](https://fiusrilanka.gov.lk/faq)]. A founder can own compliance; the immediate task does not presume a full-time hire.

The agreement should identify authority, assets, deliverables, approvals, fee/taxes, media ownership, cancellation/refunds, data roles and disputes. If later adding exclusivity or commission, define scope, duration, existing-buyer exclusions, introduction evidence, earning event, payment date and termination protection. There is no basis here to promise that a generic template or long commission tail is enforceable. A fact-specific introduction-fee judgment illustrates why the agreement and facts matter [sourced: [Supreme Court judgment](https://supremecourt.lk/wp-content/uploads/judgements/sc_appeal_223_2017.pdf)].

Property transfer requires the formal notarial process, distinct from a marketing agreement [sourced: [Prevention of Frauds amendment](https://www.documents.gov.lk/view/acts/2024/1/04-2024_E.pdf), [Registrar General services](https://www.rgd.gov.lk/web/index.php/en/services/document-land-registration/title/transactions)]. Vehicle transfer uses **MTA 6/MTA 8 [sourced: [DMT requirements](https://dmt.gov.lk/index.php?Itemid=148&id=24&lang=en&option=com_content&view=article)]**, with registration/finance documents as applicable. Obtain professional checks and transfer receipts; never treat blank transfer papers as completed ownership transfer.

## Risk register and decision rules

The ordering is a practical hypothesis, not a measured probability ranking [assumption]. The ten failure modes below have distinct early signals and an accountable response.

| Failure mode | Early warning | Response and owner |
|---|---|---|
| Building replaces selling | Missed pitch commitments while features accumulate. | Sales founder protects selling blocks; pause discretionary development. |
| No willingness to pay | Qualified proposals get compliments but no cleared funds. | Both founders examine recorded objections; revise targeting/scope once before extending the test. |
| Cash runs out before results | Forward balance cannot cover household minimums and committed delivery. | Finance owner stops discretionary spend, protects client obligations and activates outside-income/closure plan. |
| Poor inventory or unrealistic prices | Relevant buyers repeatedly reject price, condition, authority or availability. | Sales owner requests documented corrections; pause the campaign if seller refuses. |
| Cheap leads do not become appointments | High duplicate/unreachable share, weak qualification or no attendance. | Sales owner reviews source, response and qualification; performance owner pauses the next media tranche. |
| Founders drown in service | Delivery exceeds twenty founder hours per client-month or agreed response cover repeatedly fails [assumption: thresholds]. | Delivery owner enforces scope, clusters work and trials funded contractor help. |
| Consent breach or wrong-client sharing | Missing permission evidence, an opt-out followed by a message, or buyer information crosses clients. | Data owner stops the affected sending/access path, preserves records and reviews incident obligations with counsel. |
| Claims outrun proof | “Verified,” guaranteed-sale or condition claims lack a named supporting check. | Production owner blocks publication; obtain evidence or narrow wording. |
| Attribution or fee dispute | Seller contests who introduced the buyer, media cost or what the retainer included. | Sales owner uses signed scope and timestamped evidence; avoid unsupported commission claims. |
| No renewals or platform dependence | Campaigns end without a paid renewal; one account suspension removes acquisition. | Both founders interview clients, fix value delivery and maintain permissioned records/alternative distribution. Do not buy more reach to hide churn. |

**Keep going, within the existing cash cap:** the paid-client demand gate passes, the campaign has positive measured contribution, and the forward cash forecast covers committed work and the next test [assumption]. Seek paid renewal before raising fixed costs.

**Change targeting or the offer:** after **thirty qualified pitches and ten written proposals with no payment [assumption]**, review objections with decision-makers. If access/authority was wrong, repair the prospect list. If price/risk/scope was wrong, test a clearly revised version of the same service, recording the change. Allow a bounded **two-week retest only if funded [assumption]**. Do not answer by adding unrelated services or expanding geography.

**Stop or pause discretionary venture spending:** no genuine paid client by **31 January 2027 [assumption: hard operating gate]**, or earlier if unrestricted cash cannot preserve **four weeks of essential household needs plus all committed delivery/refund obligations [assumption]**. Honour existing contracts and return unearned amounts as applicable. This is a cash-control decision, not a statement that the market can never work.

**Scale only later:** measured unit contribution remains positive after paid labour, renewals support the next period, response/quality remain within contract, and the hiring gate passes [assumption]. A signature, follower milestone or gross revenue figure cannot substitute for those checks.
